## Executive Summary

**HELIX-COE** is an end-to-end commercial due diligence (CDD) and total addressable market (TAM) simulation workbench engineered specifically for healthcare and life sciences private equity case teams. Developed to bridge advanced technical engineering (React, Tailwind CSS, quantitative simulation modeling) with rigorous management consulting frameworks, the platform models macro-healthcare shifts—such as GLP-1 comorbidity spillover and CMS Medicare fee-schedule compression—across global Pharma, MedTech, Provider, Payer, and Digital Health markets.

---

## Key Modules & Consulting Frameworks

1. **Epidemiological Patient Funnel & Revenue Bridge (Exhibit 1):**
   - Bottom-up patient flow model scaling from total disease prevalence (TAM) through diagnosed cohorts to treated/adherent populations.
   - Interactive SVG waterfall bridge quantifying 5-year addressable market growth driven by aging demographics, diagnostic awareness, GLP-1 volume headwinds/tailwinds, and CMS reimbursement haircuts.
2. **Healthcare Value Chain Profit Pool Marimekko (Exhibit 2):**
   - 5-domain Marimekko matrix contrasting revenue share (width) against EBITDA margins (height) across Pharmaceuticals, MedTech, Providers, Payers, and Digital Health ($68B+ ecosystem).
3. **CapitalIQ M&A Comps & rNPV Valuation Screener (Exhibit 3):**
   - Live benchmarking table tracking EV/EBITDA multiples, R&D intensity, revenue CAGRs, and FDA 510(k)/PMA clinical catalysts across peer companies (e.g., *ResMed*, *Inspire Medical*, *iRhythm*, *Dexcom*, *Guardant Health*).
   - Dynamic Risk-Adjusted Net Present Value (rNPV) and MOIC calculation engine incorporating FDA Phase III / regulatory Probability of Technical and Regulatory Success (PTRS) and WACC discount curves.
4. **Reactive Investment Committee (IC) Verdict Memo:**
   - Automatically synthesizes MECE 3-pillar investment theses and adjusts deal verdicts (*Proceed to Confirmatory Diligence* vs. *Price-Sensitive / Pass*) based on real-time slider stress-testing.
5. **Client Deliverable Automation:**
   - Built-in 1-Click CSV Databook exporter and Markdown/IC summary copy generator for zero-defect executive outputs.
